# Director's Brief: "Keynote"

## What the viewer should feel
**Anticipation → admiration → trust → invitation.** The feeling of watching a well-made product launch, except the product is a person.

- **0–3 s, anticipation.** "Introducing…" A beautiful object on a quiet white stage. Calm confidence.
- **3–15 s, admiration.** Recognition, a breakthrough, speed, research. Each chapter is a single clear claim with a single number.
- **15–25.5 s, trust.** Breadth: five things he built, the community he leads, the foundation he earned. The music lifts here.
- **25.5–30 s, invitation.** The mission in his own words, then how to reach him.

## Rules of clarity
1. **One chapter, one claim.** The chapter label names the topic, the statement makes the claim, and the visual proves it.
2. **Read time.** Each statement holds at least 1.5× its reading time. Support text is 8 words or fewer.
3. **Numbers are heroes.** 63×, 0.03%, 9, < 3 s, 77% and ~90% are the largest things in their frames.
4. **Calm motion.** Soft springs and dollies. Nothing jitters, nothing slams. Clarity reads as confidence.
