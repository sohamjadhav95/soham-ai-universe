# Director's brief: "Your model is lying to you."

## What the viewer should feel
**Doubt → tension → relief → admiration → purpose → invitation.**

- **0–11 s, doubt and tension.** The viewer's trusted readout is wrong. The error is small, invisible and everywhere. Pix gets snapped.
- **11–20 s, relief.** Someone measured it properly. The error almost vanishes, and we learn the name.
- **20–43 s, admiration.** He's fast, he ships, he publishes, he leads. Each feature is a tiny story with one number.
- **43–50.75 s, purpose and invitation.** The mission in his own words ("Building AI for good faith of humanity."), then the contact card: "Let's build together."

## Rules
1. **The voice leads.** Every visual beat starts on its word (from `lib/cues.js`), never on a guess.
2. **One accent word per line,** in italic serif and hot orange.
3. **Numbers are the protagonists:** 10.19% → 0.03%, 63×, < 3 s, ~90%, Twice.
4. **Professional, punchy, playful.** Pix is charming, not cartoonish: no limbs and no slapstick, only expressions and squash and stretch.
